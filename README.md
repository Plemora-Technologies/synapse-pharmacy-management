# Synapse by Plemora

Synapse is pharmacy management software for independent pharmacies. It runs the counter, the stockroom and the back office from one system, and one account covers every branch.

Built by [Plemora Technologies](https://plemora.net). Used by pharmacies in East Africa, Nigeria and Pakistan.

## What Synapse does

- **Point of sale:** retail and wholesale selling at the counter, with receipts and customer records.
- **Stock control:** batch and expiry tracking, low stock and expiry alerts, and dead stock reports.
- **Purchasing:** supplier records, purchase orders and goods received.
- **Multi-branch:** all branches in one account, with transfers of stock between them.
- **Reports:** sales, profit by category, staff performance and supplier analysis.
- **Users and roles:** separate logins for owners, managers and counter staff.
